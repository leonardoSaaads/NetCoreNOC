# v0.9.1 — Gate 4: the close channel, and the measurement

Workstreams 2 and 3.

---

## 1. The close channel

`POST /api/situations/{sid}/close` accepts an optional verdict and, when given one, records **the
same label the feedback endpoint would** — same bag, same digest, same coverage, same scope
fingerprint, same exclusion discipline — with `acquisition_channel = 'close'`.

| Claim | Control |
|---|---|
| a close-recorded verdict is `close`, a card-recorded verdict is `organic` | `test_a_close_recorded_verdict_carries_the_close_channel` |
| the two labels are identical in **every other respect** | `test_a_close_carrying_a_verdict_records_the_same_label_a_card_would` compares `verdict`, `member_count`, `excluded_count`, `excluded_truncated`, `coverage`, `capture_provenance` and `scope_restricted` field by field |
| closing **without** a verdict is unchanged | `test_closing_without_a_verdict_is_unchanged` — no body, `{}`, and `{"verdict": null}` all answer `200 {"status":"closed"}` and write **zero** rows to `feedback` |
| a verdict needs `feedback.write` as well as `situation.close` | `test_a_close_with_a_verdict_needs_feedback_write_as_well` — under a stored policy granting one and not the other, the close **with** a verdict is 403 and the close **without** one is 200 |

The identity in row two is what the channel column's value rests on: if labels from the two channels
differed in some other way nobody intended, a per-channel comparison would be measuring that
difference rather than the population.

**One request stays one audit row.** The verdict rides along in the existing `situation.close`
row's `details`; no audit action, capability, route or served path was added.

### 1.1 The gesture is deferred, and the consequence is stated rather than buried

DECISIONS #130. Adding *Confirm & close* and *Split & close* is technically two `el("button", …)`
calls — no panel, no modal, no new file — and it would put **five near-identical click targets in
one row**, two differing from their neighbours only in a trailing word, on the one path in this
product where a mis-click is not an annoyance but a **silently wrong label**. That is the failure
`FEEDBACK-PATH-0.7.5-DRAFT.md` §1.1 exists to prevent, and a release inserted for label integrity
should not raise the mis-click rate on the label path in order to raise the label count.

> **The shipped UI therefore writes `organic` on every row.** This release adds the channel's
> *mechanism* and none of its *volume*: it raises the **informativeness** of a label and not the
> **rate**. The endpoint is what a rebuilt UI will use, and the column now has a second value that
> is defined, tested and reported per channel the day anything writes it.

## 2. The measurement

### 2.1 `make bias-report` — informativeness, channels, and the prize

New sections, all deterministic and aggregates-only:

* **`ASSERTED NEGATIVE PAIRS`**, total and per label — *the quantity that was zero for every corpus
  that has ever existed*;
* the **plain versus partial** split breakdown, and `|marked|` relative to bag size in tenths,
  because marking eight of nine is a different assertion from marking one of nine;
* **`pairs left UNASSERTED in those bags`**, printed beside the negatives so that v0.10.0 cannot
  read *"not asserted positive"* as *"asserted negative"*;
* the **remainder assertion**, `offered` and `taken`, both **structurally zero** while the shipped UI
  has no control for it — named rather than quietly omitted, because a rate over an affordance
  nobody was given is not a measurement of operators;
* an **acquisition-channel** section repeating labels, verdicts, partial splits, asserted negatives,
  scope restriction and client reports per channel;
* **`JUDGEMENT NOT CAPTURED`** — situations closed with and without a verdict.

### 2.2 `make agreement-report` — every existing conditioning, per channel

`by_channel`, then the five conditional cuts (`by_size`, `by_storm`, `by_mixedness`, `by_scope`,
`by_coverage`) repeated **within** each channel. The cuts are computed by one `_cuts()` helper used
for both the overall figures and the per-channel ones, so the two can never drift apart.

**The fixture makes the point rather than decorating it.** Four of its twelve labels are `close`,
and they are deliberately *not* a random quarter — they skew toward `confirm` and toward larger
bags, which is the selection the channel is suspected of having:

```
  organic                 n=    8  confirmed=    4    50.0%
  close                   n=    4  confirmed=    4   100.0%
```

A blended headline would read 66.7 % and would describe nobody.

### 2.3 `make shadow-report` — the floors re-evaluated

```
  ADDITIONAL OBSERVATIONS — not floors, and not substituted for one:
  split AND mixed bags      (no floor)        20
  asserted negative pairs   (no floor)         0
```

`SECURITY-REVIEW-0.9.0.md` §5.4 argued the floor should have been over `split ∧ mixed` rather than
`split`. That opinion is **reported and floors nothing**. Reporting a better quantity is honest;
replacing a registered floor after the data has been seen is exactly what pre-registration exists to
prevent, and `HONEST-JUDGE-0.10-DRAFT.md` §5 is where it is accepted or rejected — in advance.

**The v0.9.0 pre-registration is untouched**: `bb5bff851588837aa07f21c54b5301f7ada5fec3f8017a5ca4e9d7f7da2cbaef`.

## 3. The three fixture diffs, explained

All three reports are byte-for-byte gates. Each was re-frozen **by hand, having read the diff**.

### 3.1 `tests/fixtures/bias-report.txt`

The fixture gained a fourth situation (`sid_d`, three members, three promoted pairs straddling the
threshold) carrying a **partial split**, and a **close-channel** confirm on `sid_b`. Without them the
whole informativeness section would print zeros and the gate would notice nothing about the feature
the release exists for.

| Change | Cause |
|---|---|
| `pairs, dataset` 5 → 8; `observations, sink` 6 → 9 | the new situation's three members and three pairs |
| `incumbent linked` 3 → 5 (60.0 % → 62.5 %); `storm` 40.0 % → 25.0 % | denominators grew; no capture behaviour changed |
| `confirm` 2 → 3, `split` 2 → 3, total 4 → 6 | the two new labels |
| whole `INFORMATIVENESS` section | new |
| whole `ACQUISITION CHANNELS` section | new |
| whole `JUDGEMENT NOT CAPTURED` section | new |
| `top operator share` 66.7 % → 60.0 %; latency `n=3` → `n=5` | two more attributed labels |
| coverage `full` 2 → 3, `none` 0 → 1 | the new bags |
| `provenance: current` 3 → 5; `acquisition: organic` 3 → 4, `close` 0 → 1 | the two new labels |

**The arithmetic in the new section checks out by hand.** One member marked of a three-member bag:
`m·r = 1 × 2 = 2` asserted negatives, `r(r−1)/2 + m(m−1)/2 = 1 + 0 = 1` unasserted, and
`2 + 1 = 3 = n(n−1)/2`. The report prints exactly `2` and `1`.

### 3.2 `tests/fixtures/agreement-report.txt`

**Purely additive** — two new sections and no existing line changed, because the channel is a new
dimension over the same twelve bags. `PLAN` gained a seventh field; four rows read `close`.

### 3.3 `tests/fixtures/shadow-report.txt`

**Purely additive** — five lines and a heading inside `SUFFICIENCY`. No existing figure moved,
because nothing about the corpus, the fit or the metrics changed.
