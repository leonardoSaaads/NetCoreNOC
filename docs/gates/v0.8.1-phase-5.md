## 4. The label's life, end to end

`test_the_whole_life_of_a_label` — one narrative, six steps: capture fills the sink; a verdict
promotes; **past the operational retention** the verdict and bag survive while `situation_alarm` and
`link` are collected; **past the training boundary** four passes delete nothing; **past the audit
bound** the label, its bag and its features are collected together and counted
(`audit_swept["labels"] == 1`, `audit_swept["pairs"] == promoted`); and the retained situation shell
is collected by the next ordinary pass.
