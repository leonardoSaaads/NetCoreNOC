## 2. The six cuts, and the one that carries the argument

| Cut | Question it answers that the aggregate cannot |
|---|---|
| **mixed vs uniform** | *Was there a decision to get wrong?* A uniform bag's every pair fell on the same side of the threshold, so the confirm says nothing about the scorer's judgement |
| bag size | confirmation strength decays with size |
| storm | in a storm the champion is right about something it could not have got wrong |
| visibility scope | that operator judged a partial view, and the resulting noise is **systematic** |
| operator | a headline over three people is three people's opinion |
| capture provenance | `current` and `legacy_capture`, **separately, never averaged** |

Coverage is reported as a seventh cut. A bag with `coverage='none'` or `'empty'` has no promoted
pair and appears in the `no-pairs` row of every other cut — **counted, never silently dropped**.

### Operators are anonymised — DECISIONS #120

The per-operator cut prints `operator 1 … operator N`, ordered by bag count and then by a digest of
the reference. Printing `principal_ref` would turn a bias measurement into a per-employee
performance report generated by a tool nobody was told would generate one. The **spread** — the
quantity the cut exists for — survives anonymisation intact.

It is structural, not procedural: the alias map is built in `agreement.collect` and the raw reference
is never placed in the returned document, so the renderer **cannot** print one by mistake.
`test_the_report_never_prints_an_operator_identity` asserts absence from the rendered text *and*
from `repr(measurements)`.

## 3. Determinism, and the generator that makes it hold

The report is a byte-for-byte gate, so the bootstrap could not use `random`: `random.Random` is
deterministic for a seed *on a given implementation*, and this file must survive an interpreter
upgrade. A **64-bit LCG is written out in `agreement.py`** — six lines, no dependency, reproducible
forever.

```
BOOTSTRAP_RESAMPLES        1000
BOOTSTRAP_SEED             20090409
MIN_INCIDENTS_FOR_INTERVAL 10
```

Resampling is over **incidents** — `COALESCE(situation.merged_into, feedback.situation_id)` — not
over bags and never over pairs, per the pre-registration §5.5. **Below ten incidents the bootstrap is
refused outright** and the report prints `[interval n/a]`: resampling a handful of clusters produces
an interval that looks like a measurement and is an artefact of a handful of numbers.

## 4. The gate fixture

`tests/fixtures/agreement-report.txt`, built from twelve labelled incidents — one more than the
bootstrap's refusal threshold, so the interval is exercised rather than skipped. It deliberately
carries the awkward cases: bags in six size buckets, three mixed / six uniform-accept / two
uniform-reject / one pairless, storm and quiet and *partly*-storm bags, a scoped label, three named
operators plus one unattributed, and a `legacy_capture` row that must never reach the headline.

Headline over the fixture:

```
-- THE HEADLINE ----------------------------------------------
  champion agreement      n=   12  confirmed=    8    66.7%  [ 33.3,  91.7]

-- by MIXED vs UNIFORM bag (the strongest cut) ---------------
  mixed                   n=    3  confirmed=    2    66.7%  [interval n/a]
  uniform-accept          n=    6  confirmed=    5    83.3%  [interval n/a]
  uniform-reject          n=    2  confirmed=    0     0.0%  [interval n/a]
  no-pairs                n=    1  confirmed=    1   100.0%  [interval n/a]

-- by capture provenance (NEVER averaged) --------------------
  current                 n=   12  confirmed=    8    66.7%  [ 33.3,  91.7]
  legacy_capture          n=    1  confirmed=    0     0.0%  [interval n/a]
```

`test_legacy_rows_are_never_averaged_into_the_headline` asserts the arithmetic that makes this
non-accidental: `bags_all == bags + 1`, the headline's `n` equals the `current` count, and the
legacy row appears only in the provenance section.

**Guard the guard**: `test_the_gate_goes_red_when_the_corpus_changes` injects one new labelled
situation and asserts the report moves. A gate that survived that would be measuring nothing.

## 5. The number over a real corpus — and what it does and does not mean

Run over the Phase 0 reading (b) corpus: every eval scenario driven through one engine, then every
situation it formed given a verdict, labels spread over fourteen days.

```
-- population ------------------------------------------------
  labelled bags, all provenance               41
  labelled bags, provenance=current           41
  merge-aware incidents among them            37
  distinct operators                           3
  bags with no promoted pair                   6
  promoted pairs behind them              194341
  verdict: confirm                            28
  verdict: split                              13

-- THE HEADLINE ----------------------------------------------
  champion agreement      n=   41  confirmed=   28    68.3%  [ 54.8,  81.1]

-- by MIXED vs UNIFORM bag (the strongest cut) ---------------
  mixed                   n=    5  confirmed=    4    80.0%  [interval n/a]
  uniform-accept          n=    6  confirmed=    4    66.7%  [interval n/a]
  uniform-reject          n=   24  confirmed=   15    62.5%  [ 41.7,  83.3]
  no-pairs                n=    6  confirmed=    5    83.3%  [interval n/a]

-- by bag size -----------------------------------------------
  0 (empty bag)           n=    4  confirmed=    2    50.0%  [interval n/a]
  1                       n=   29  confirmed=   20    69.0%  [ 51.7,  86.2]
  6-10                    n=    1  confirmed=    1   100.0%  [interval n/a]
  11-50                   n=    2  confirmed=    2   100.0%  [interval n/a]
  51+                     n=    5  confirmed=    3    60.0%  [interval n/a]
```

> **⚠ THIS 68.3 % IS NOT A MEASUREMENT OF OPERATORS AND MUST NEVER BE QUOTED AS ONE.**
>
> The verdicts were assigned by a **mechanical rule** — every third situation `split`, the rest
> `confirm` — declared in Phase 0 §2(b) before the report existed. A rule that splits one situation
> in three produces a confirm rate of 66.7 % **by arithmetic**, and 68.3 % is that rule, plus
> rounding over 41 bags. It measures the driver.
>
> What it *does* establish is everything this gate is for: the query runs at real scale (194 341
> promoted pairs), the cuts populate, the clustered interval computes over 37 incidents and
> **refuses** on the cells with too few, and the whole thing is deterministic. The number this
> release publishes as a *finding* is the one a real deployment produces; this one is a
> load-bearing test of the instrument.
>
> This is directive 11's *"ambiguity about whether a number is trustworthy resolves toward reporting
> the uncertainty"*, applied to the release's own headline.

**One thing the synthetic corpus does show honestly**, because it is structural rather than
label-dependent: **the mixed population is 5 bags out of 41** — 12 %. Whatever an operator's real
confirm rate turns out to be, only about an eighth of it would be about a decision the champion
could have got wrong. That is the shape of the headroom, and it does not depend on who did the
labelling.
