### The two required critical notes, and three more

1. **The gate is complete for *registration*; that is not correctness of what it guards.** A route
   can no longer be undeclared; it can still be **wrongly** declared. Every `ROUTE_SCOPE` entry is a
   human judgement, and `ROUTE_SCOPE` remains **descriptive rather than injected** — v0.7.2's
   deliberate choice (DECISIONS #80) and still a ROADMAP line.
2. **An empty `DEBT_ALLOWLIST` does not mean the codebase is well factored.** It means no module
   exceeds a line count, which is a proxy. §4.2 names what a reviewer would look at next —
   `api/perimeter.py` (361), which it argues **should not** be split and should instead get a
   recorded reason for staying whole; and `scoring.py`/`learn.py`, both one line under the guard and
   therefore about to become an obstacle rather than a signal — explicitly as an opinion rather than
   a commitment, and explicitly excluding `engine.py`.
3. **`/openapi.json` is served unauthenticated** (§4.3). Found while building F41's allowlist,
   recorded rather than fixed. Severity assessed honestly: low, and not nothing — no data and no
   credential, every route it names still enforced, but it hands an unauthenticated reader the exact
   shape of the write surface.
4. **A guard in this suite counts mentions, not calls** (§4.4). Documenting the F40 fix in a
   docstring tripped `test_add_api_route_is_confined_to_the_static_asset_allowlist`; the prose was
   reworded rather than the test.
5. **This was not a full re-review of the attack surface** (§4.5). Stated so nothing here reads as
   evidence that F1–F39's controls were re-tested beyond what CI asserts.

Items 3 and 4 are also `docs/ROADMAP.md` lines, per directive 5.
