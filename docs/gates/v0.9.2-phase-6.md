## 8. The intentional behaviour changes, counted against the list

`SCOPE-0.9.2.md` §2 enumerated four. All four are present, and **no fifth was found**:

1. `make bias-report` leads with the disagreement, then both tiers, then the three scope populations.
2. `make shadow-report` labels its asserted-negative line `(server)` and prints `(client)` beside it.
3. `make dataset-stats` splits `feedback_member` by source; the total is still printed.
4. `make migrate` derives `excluded_reconciled` on labels that already carry a marking.

The two frozen report fixtures are the mechanism that would have caught a fifth: any unlisted change
to either report is a byte diff in a gate, and both diffs were read line by line before re-freezing.
