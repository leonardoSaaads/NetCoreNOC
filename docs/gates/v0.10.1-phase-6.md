### The three critical notes, in one line each

1. **The guard I am least confident in** is `_cycle_members`'s bound and the test behind it: it
   tests a private function because no reachable input can violate the precondition, its control
   does **not** discriminate an off-by-one (mutation A13 survived, equivalent), and the bound
   converts a hang into a *wrong number* rather than into a correct one.
2. **Could B1 have moved a frozen number unnoticed? Yes.** Neither frozen corpus contains a single
   merge edge, so the byte-for-byte gate **cannot discriminate** one-hop from transitive — measured,
   and demonstrated by mutations A8, A9, M6 and M7, which all leave both reports byte-identical.
   What verifies B1 is the structural guard and three purpose-built fixtures, not the frozen reports.
3. **The coverage figure is not reproducible**, and A3 only removed one of two causes. Two runs of
   the pinned command on the same tree give 96.21 % and 96.10 %. Consequence stated rather than
   buried: a release gate of the form *"coverage at or above X"* is not well-defined on this suite,
   and has not been for as long as the fuzzing has existed.
